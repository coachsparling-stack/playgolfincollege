What's inside

The right way to start.

Foundations is a year-long membership on Lighthouse Golf Parent, built for any junior golf parent who wants to do this the right way from the beginning. Everything I teach families in Elite and Advisory, in a form you run yourself.

01
All four books

Becoming Elite, The Lighthouse Golf Parent, Start Here and Building Elite Programs, included with membership.

02
The 10 Pillars

The framework behind The Lighthouse Golf Parent: the ten things a golf parent controls that shape how a player develops.

03
Scripts and templates

What to say on the car ride home, how to structure practice, and the letters and résumés that get a college coach's attention.

04
Parent Assessment

Know where you are as a golf parent right now, and what to improve on to help your kid.

05
Members newsletter

Regular guidance from me on development, tournaments, recruiting and the parent's role.

06
Ask Coach Spar

Coming soon: answers to your questions, in my voice, whenever you need them.

Who it's for

  • Parents of younger players who want to get the environment right before the stakes rise.
  • Families who want the plan and will run it themselves, without weekly or monthly contact with me.
  • Anyone considering Elite or Advisory who wants to start with the foundation first. Foundations is included in both.
