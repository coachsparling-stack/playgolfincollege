I'm Brad Sparling. Most people call me Spar.

I've been the college coach reading your emails, the recruiter who placed 165 players, the swing teacher, the high school coach of back-to-back state champions, and the dad of three Division I golfers. For 25 years I've worked only with junior golfers and their families, and I've written four books on how to do it well.
